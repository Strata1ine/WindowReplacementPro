# Customer disclosure policy

## Public website

The public website presents supplier-neutral product information, design and performance guidance, Window Replacement Pro references, and category-level selection breadth. It is not a sourcing directory and does not disclose supplier relationships, wholesale cost, markup, or margin.

A supplier-backed product can receive a public page only after its display name, slug, reference, summary, specifications, and media have been explicitly reviewed for confidentiality. Products without an approved public identity remain internal or category-only.

Raw source documents, source URLs, internal model numbers, supplier identities, and provenance objects remain internal. Public production media uses neutral filenames and paths; the provenance chain remains in the internal evidence library.

## Formal written quotation before contract

The written quotation identifies the actual manufacturer, exact model, performance ratings, glazing and configuration, warranty, installation scope, exclusions, and installed price before the customer accepts the contract.

Exact source documents can be provided with the written quotation when appropriate. Supplier branding is not removed or altered to create public-facing documents.

## Approved wording

Product systems are selected from established manufacturers. The exact manufacturer, model, performance documentation and warranty are identified in your written quotation.

This statement should appear once in the product specification context rather than as repeated defensive boilerplate.
