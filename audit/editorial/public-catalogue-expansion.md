## Held families

- **Bay and bow windows:** Insufficient reviewed canonical-product and product-specific public-media coverage.
- **Steel entry doors:** No internally published steel-door record currently satisfies the complete public evidence and hero-media gate.
- **Double doors, sidelites and transom door systems:** Configuration evidence is not yet complete enough for independent product pages.
- **Three-panel arched entry doors:** The cleanest reviewed hero is region-limited, while the remaining media is not reliable enough for a public page.
- **Internal-blind and venting door glass:** No reviewed public candidate currently has both sufficient normalized evidence and approved product-specific media.
- **Stacking and oversized patio doors:** Current reviewed public candidates do not support a sufficiently specific, media-backed standalone page.

## Publication policy

The exact manufacturer and model remain private until identified in the written quotation.
